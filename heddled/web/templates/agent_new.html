Agents / New agent

An agent is an assistant that answers questions and does jobs for you. You tell it how to behave, give it a few things it's allowed to do, and decide what it must ask you about first.

1 · What is it called?

Lowercase, words joined by underscores. People won't usually see this.

One sentence. This is how you'll recognise it in a list later.

2 · How should it behave?

Write it like you're briefing a new colleague on their first day. Say what they handle, what tone to use, and — most usefully — what to do when they don't know the answer.
