Jarvis

Settings

Give it a goal and a budget and it works on its own: it writes the tools it needs, writes agents to use them, runs them, and keeps going until it says it is finished or it runs out of money or steps. Everything it makes lives in its own tree until you move it across. Discard a run and what it made goes with it.

{#- Said once, plainly, on the screen where a run is started — not tucked into a tooltip on a button somebody has already pressed. -#}

This is not how you build something you mean to rely on. Jarvis decides for itself what to build and how, spends money doing it, and nobody reads what it wrote until you do. Use it to find out what a goal would take, then read the result before you trust a line of it.

What it can and cannot reach

  • It writes only into jarvis/. Your own agents/ and tools/ are a different tree, read through a different registry, and nothing in Jarvis holds a path to them.
  • It may ask your agents, never change them. Questions arrive on the jarvis channel, so a policy can refuse them by name, and every approval gate and budget on that agent still applies.
  • Python it writes runs in a child process with no API keys, no access to the store, and no way to import Heddled — capped on memory, CPU and time.
  • Nothing crosses over until you promote it, one thing at a time, and promoting refuses to overwrite anything of yours.
